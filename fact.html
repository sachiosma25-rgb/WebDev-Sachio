5 FACTS ABOUT UNIVERSITY OF MALAYA

Oldest University In Malaysia

Founded originally as the King Edward VII College of Medicine on September 28, 1905, in Singapore, it later merged with Raffles College in 1949 and was established as a national university on January 1, 1962

Massive Campus Size

Located in the southwest of Kuala Lumpur, the main campus covers a vast area of about 922 acres (roughly 373 hectares).

Politician Nursery

UM has produced five former Prime Ministers of Malaysia, including Tun Dr. Mahathir Mohamad.

Motto and Emblem

The university's coat of arms features the motto "Ilmu Puncha Kemajuan," which translates from Malay to "Knowledge is the Source of Progress"

Top Global Standing

It consistently ranks as Malaysia's top-performing public university and maintains high placements in international league tables like the QS World University
